THE STORY

About The Shattered Convergence

Across realities, fractures are spreading. Events once confined to separate worlds begin leaving echoes where they do not belong, and the boundaries that kept one life from touching another are no longer holding.

The heroes of the Convergence Saga have each faced powers capable of reshaping everything they know. Now the consequences of those struggles begin reaching beyond individual worlds. Patterns repeat. Impossible connections surface. Questions that once seemed personal reveal a scale none of them were prepared to confront.

The Shattered Convergence brings the saga toward the mystery waiting beneath all four books: why these worlds are breaking, what connects their seemingly separate stories, and what may happen when the walls between them finally fail.
