THE STORY

About Meteor's Chosen

Jaden Duin expects a meteor shower, not the end of everything familiar. When the sky tears open and his family is swept into an impossible world, survival becomes the only plan that matters.

Aethermoor is beautiful, dangerous, and alive in ways Earth never was. Strange energies move through its landscapes, ancient cultures guard truths Jaden does not understand, and something about his family's arrival feels less like an accident with every passing day.

As the Duins search for answers and a way forward, unfamiliar abilities begin surfacing among them. Jaden's greatest concern is simple: keep his family together. But in a world where even reality follows unfamiliar rules, protecting the people he loves may require him to become something he never knew he could be.
