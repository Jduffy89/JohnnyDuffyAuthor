THE STORY

About Chronicles of Rei

Rei Carter and his friends thought they understood their lives, their families, and one another. Then the impossible tears through the ordinary and pulls them into a world where magic is real, danger waits beyond every familiar path, and the answers they seek may change everything they believed about themselves.

Separated from the lives they knew and forced to adapt to powers they barely understand, Rei and his friends must depend on one another as they cross an unfamiliar land filled with ancient mysteries, unexpected allies, and enemies whose motives reach further than any of them realize.

For Rei, the journey becomes something more personal. The deeper he travels into this strange world, the harder it becomes to ignore the feeling that he did not arrive there by accident—and that discovering the way forward may first require discovering who he truly is.
